Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Drivo ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and authentication credentials. We use Clerk for authentication services.
  • Profile Information: Profile picture, preferred language, and other information you choose to add to your profile.
  • Ride Information: Pickup and drop-off locations, ride preferences, and trip history.
  • Payment Information: Payment card details and billing address. Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store your full card number on our servers.
  • Communications: Information you provide when you contact us for support, report issues, or otherwise communicate with us.
  • Driver Information (if applicable): Vehicle details, driver's license number, insurance information, and background check results for driver verification.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: We collect your device's GPS location in real-time during active rides and when the app is in the foreground. This is essential for matching riders with nearby drivers, providing navigation, and ensuring safety.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: App features used, interaction patterns, crash logs, and performance data.
  • IP Address: Your IP address is collected for security, fraud prevention, and geographic service delivery.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, operate, and maintain the ride-hailing service, including matching riders with drivers, processing payments, and providing real-time navigation.
  • Safety and Security: To verify user identities, detect fraudulent activity, ensure driver compliance, and maintain the safety of all users.
  • Communication: To send ride updates, receipts, service notifications, and respond to your inquiries.
  • Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, troubleshoot issues, and improve the Service's functionality and user experience.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.
  • Marketing: To send promotional communications (with your consent, where required by law). You may opt out at any time.

4. Location Information

Location data is critical to Drivo's core functionality. We collect and process location information as follows:

  • Ride Matching: Your real-time location helps us find nearby drivers and estimate arrival times.
  • Navigation: During active rides, we track location to provide turn-by-turn navigation to drivers and ride progress to riders.
  • Safety Features: Location data enables safety features such as ride sharing, emergency assistance, and trip verification.
  • Pickup/Drop-off: We store your pickup and drop-off locations to improve service and provide trip history.
Important: You can control location sharing through your device settings. However, disabling location access will prevent you from using the Service, as real-time location is essential for ride-hailing functionality.

  • Stripe: Payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Ride Partners: When you request a ride, the driver receives your name, pickup location, and profile picture. When a driver accepts your ride, you receive the driver's name, photo, vehicle information, and real-time location.
  • Service Providers: We share information with third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf (payment processing, analytics, cloud hosting).
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal processes, court orders, or government requests.
  • Safety: We may share information if we believe it is necessary to prevent imminent harm to health or safety, or to protect the rights and property of Drivo and its users.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account Information: Retained for the duration of your account and for 30 days after account deletion.
  • Ride History: Retained for 12 months for service improvement and dispute resolution.
  • Payment Information: Transaction records retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations.
  • Location Data: Real-time location data is deleted after ride completion. Aggregated location data may be retained for analytics.
  • Support Communications: Retained for 24 months to improve customer service.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data transmission is encrypted using TLS/SSL.
  • Payment card data is tokenized and never stored on our servers (handled by Stripe's PCI-compliant infrastructure).
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.
  • We conduct regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • User authentication is managed through Clerk's secure infrastructure.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but will promptly notify you of any breaches as required by law.
